01
The problem

A Pages Router site on Bootstrap/SCSS, cross-browser bugs, slow loads, and every content edit routed through engineering.

02
The approach

Route-by-route migration to App Router behind flags, a Tailwind component layer, and pre-commit ESLint plus build validation so regressions stopped shipping.

02 · Reckonsys client work · Solo build

A marketing-attribution platform, from an empty repo

A client needed to prove which ad spend actually turned into revenue — not clicks, not "engagement", closed deals. Leads lived in a CRM, spend lived in the ad platforms, and nobody could join the two. I built the whole app from scratch, on my own.

My roleSole developerat Reckonsys
  • Configurable funnels — lead → contacted → appointment → deal
  • CRM ingestion from a simple JSON event per stage
  • Native paid-source connections for spend and campaign metrics
  • Matching server-side events back to the spend behind them
  • Funnel, revenue and campaign-performance dashboards in React + Recharts
01
The problem

Ad platforms grade themselves on engagement. The business needed revenue per source — and a way to settle the sales-vs-marketing argument with data.

02
The build

Every layer, from the first commit: the funnel model, CRM and ad-source ingestion, attribution matching, and the product UI on top.

03
The dashboards

Spend against leads against approvals, true revenue per lead source, goals per channel, and team views sales managers actually open.
